## Article Promo

The sustainability model most companies follow was designed backwards. Start with the destination, and everything becomes a cost. Start with operations, and everything becomes a margin improvement. I wrote about why the ambition was never the problem — the infrastructure was.

---

## Note 1

A fifty-thousand-euro sustainability report that nobody reads is not a sustainability strategy. It's a receipt.

---

## Note 2

The pattern is so consistent it should be a template: announce the commitment, hire the consultant, produce the PDF, quietly shelve it at the next budget review. The problem was never motivation. It was sequencing. Modern sustainability starts with operations, not ambition.

---

## Note 3

Sustainability is just another way to say good business. The moment you accept that, the economics flip. It stops being something you fund and becomes something that funds itself.
