"What a sustainability questionnaire actually asks you"
— and what it means in plain language

**Slide 2:**
THEY ASK: "Report Scope 1 and Scope 2 GHG emissions by source, in tCO2e, using a location-based methodology."

THEY MEAN: How much gas and electricity do you use? Check your bills.

**Slide 3:**
THEY ASK: "Provide waste generation data by type and disposal method, including diversion rates."

THEY MEAN: What do you throw away, how much of it, and does any of it get recycled? Your waste hauler has this.

**Slide 4:**
THEY ASK: "Describe your water stewardship approach, including withdrawal volumes and discharge quality."

THEY MEAN: How much water do you use? Check your water bill.

**Slide 5:**
THEY ASK: "Detail your Scope 3 upstream emissions, including purchased goods and services, and transportation."

THEY MEAN: What do you buy, who delivers it, and how far does it travel? Your purchase orders and logistics contracts.

**Slide 6 (Close):**
The data they're asking for?
You're already sitting on most of it.

You just never had a reason to organise it this way.
