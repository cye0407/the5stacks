What to do with this

  1. Count your "High risk" suppliers. Those are your single points of failure.
  2. For each high-risk supplier, start one conversation this month: ask about their capacity, constraints, and what's changing on their end.
  3. For any supplier where "Alternative?" is blank or "don't know," that's research to do before you need it.
  4. Revisit this map quarterly. Risks shift. A low-risk supplier in January can become high-risk by June.
