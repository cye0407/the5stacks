Energy Waste Quick-Scan Checklist

The 5 Stacks Framework

Walk the floor. Check each item. Estimate the monthly cost of any waste you find. Pick the biggest one and fix it this week. At current energy prices, even small fixes pay back fast.

Time required: ~30 minutes on the floor + 15 minutes calculating.
