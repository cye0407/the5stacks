# Five Stacks — Competitive Analysis: Stacks 2–5

*February 2026*

---

## What Is Five Stacks?

A progressive sustainability operating system for SMEs. Five capability layers, each unlocking the next:

| Stack | Name | Capability | Core Question |
|-------|------|-----------|---------------|
| 1 | Metrics (Visibility) | See your operation | What's actually happening? |
| 2 | Efficiency (Margin) | Fix leaks | Where are we bleeding margin? |
| 3 | Circularity (Value Capture) | Turn outputs into inputs | What value is leaving our system? |
| 4 | Resilience (Stability) | Absorb shocks + adapt | What could break us? |
| 5 | Regeneration (Positioning) | Compound advantage | How do we turn proof into premium? |

**Core thesis:** Sustainability = Adaptivity. Not compliance, not reporting — the capacity to respond appropriately to conditions and compound competitive advantage.

**Target:** Genuine SMEs (sub-250 employees, no dedicated sustainability team).

---

## The Overall Competitive Landscape

### The Big Finding

The entire SME sustainability software market is stuck at Stack 1 (measurement/reporting). Nobody covers Stacks 2–5 for SMEs.

**Mapping competitors against the Five Stacks:**

| Capability Layer | Who Covers It? |
|---|---|
| Stack 1: Visibility | Every platform — this IS the entire market |
| Stack 2: Efficiency | Cority/Intelex/Enablon (enterprise EHS only), CMMS tools (maintenance only) |
| Stack 3: Circularity | iPoint, SAP Responsible Design (enterprise only), no SME platform |
| Stack 4: Resilience | Sphera (enterprise risk only), no SME platform |
| Stack 5: Positioning | Aclymate (partial — certification-to-contract), nobody else |

**The bottom-right quadrant — SME-accessible + capability/advantage framing — is entirely empty.**

### What Every Competitor Gets Wrong

Every platform in the market frames sustainability as either:
- A compliance obligation (CSRD, SBTi, CDP, GRI)
- A carbon reduction target
- A risk to mitigate
- A reporting burden to automate

Zero platforms frame sustainability as **the mechanism through which SMEs build adaptivity, reduce costs, win contracts, access cheaper finance, and command premium positioning.**

### The Genuine SME Landscape

Products claiming "SME" focus but not genuinely SME-accessible:
- Coolset: 250–5,000 employees
- Sweep: Requires internal sustainability team
- Plan A: "Best for large companies"
- Watershed: $50K minimum

Actually SME-accessible products:
- **Sage Earth** — £9/mo (carbon-only)
- **Aclymate** — $288–$3,744/yr (carbon-only)
- **Persefoni Pro** — Free tier (carbon-only, very limited)
- **Greenly** — From $539/yr (carbon + compliance)
- **FuturePlus** — £95–£350/mo (closest to multi-dimensional, but still reporting-centric)
- **diginexESG** — $99/mo (reporting, 17 frameworks)

**None provide operational transformation, capability progression, or business outcome connection.**

### No Progressive Journey Exists

The market offers either one-time assessments (B Impact) or perpetual reporting subscriptions (everything else). No product builds capability sequentially. The B Corp BIA is the closest analog (Year 1/3/5 requirements) but it's a certification framework, not an operational system.

### The Financing Bridge Nobody Builds

73% of financial institutions now offer green/sustainable finance for SMEs. Only 2.8% of SMEs have applied. ICC/Sage research identifies a $789B green growth opportunity. Yet no sustainability platform connects a company's sustainability capability to financing terms, premium pricing, or contract qualification.

# Stack 2 — Efficiency (Margin)

> Fix leaks before adding initiatives.

## The White Space

Stack 2 sits at the intersection of three categories that don't overlap:
1. **Operational efficiency / lean tools** — ignore sustainability
2. **Carbon accounting platforms** — measure but don't drive operational action
3. **Energy management systems** — enterprise or hardware-dependent

The tool that connects operational data to efficiency recommendations, frames cost savings as sustainability ROI, and produces documented proof for financing **does not exist.**

| Tool | Pricing | SME Fit | Sustainability Link |
|------|---------|---------|-------------------|

| **Poka** | Quote-only, ~$5K–$15K/yr est. | Low — enterprise-leaning | None |
| **Augmentir** | Quote-only, ~$15K–$50K/yr est. | None | None |

**Gap:** Every credible operational efficiency platform is either manufacturing-floor specific, hardware-dependent, priced at $10K+/yr, or enterprise-only. Nothing serves a 20-person distribution company or 50-person food producer. And none connect efficiency to sustainability.

## Stack 2 Gaps Summary

### Gap 1: No Affordable SME Operational Efficiency Tool
An SME in food processing, logistics, or services with 20–200 employees has no tool that walks them through: "here is where you're wasteful, here is the ROI of fixing it, here is the sustainability impact."

### Gap 2: The Bridge Between Carbon Measurement and Operational Action Is Missing
Carbon tools tell you what your emissions are. They don't tell you what to do operationally or what it will save.

### Gap 3: Nobody Frames Efficiency as the Funding Mechanism
No product owns the narrative: "reduce operational waste → generate documented savings → use savings + evidence to access green financing → redeploy capital into deeper sustainability investment."

### Gap 4: No Documented Proof-of-Improvement Output for Financing
No self-serve tool produces a "Sustainability Efficiency Report" — baseline → change → verified outcome (cost + carbon) — that's what EU green financing and sustainability-linked loans require.

### Gap 5: The Sub-50-Employee Market Defaults to Consultants
Below ~50 employees, the market defaults to €3K–€10K sustainability consultant engagements. A tool at €99–€299/month could replace this.

## Stack 2 Positioning

**The unoccupied position:** An operational efficiency layer for SMEs that already have visibility (Stack 1), expressed in the sustainability lens — every efficiency gain is a carbon reduction, every carbon reduction is a cost saving, every documented improvement is a step toward better financing.

**Closest competitors to watch:** Carbmee (right model, enterprise-only), Sweep (financial simulation, but no operational recommendations).

# Stack 3 — Circularity (Value Capture)

> Every output can be someone's input.

## The White Space

The SME circularity tool market has a gaping hole between expensive enterprise software and free-but-useless frameworks. Almost nothing connects circular initiatives to actual financial outcomes for companies under 250 employees. 70% of manufacturers expect circular solutions to increase revenue by 2027 (Bain/WEF), but 45% have not scaled a single circular initiative — the gap is not motivation, it's the inability to see the financial case before committing.

## Category 1: Circular Economy Assessment Platforms

| Tool | Pricing | SME Fit | Financial Quantification? |
|------|---------|---------|--------------------------|
| **Circulytics (Ellen MacArthur Foundation)** | Free — but shut down Aug 2023 | Was moderate (data-heavy) | No — scored circularity, didn't value it |
| **Circular IQ / CTI Tool** | Enterprise, undisclosed | None | No |
| **Material Circularity Indicator (EMF)** | Free Excel download | Yes (very limited) | No |

**Key insight:** The Ellen MacArthur Foundation itself walked away from individual assessments — too expensive to do properly at scale. Direct invitation for a self-serve replacement. CTI framework is public but the tool running it is paywalled for large corporations.

## Category 2: Industrial Symbiosis / Waste Marketplaces

| Tool | Pricing | SME Fit | Financial Quantification? |
|------|---------|---------|--------------------------|
| **Excess Materials Exchange (NL)** | Undisclosed, advisory model | Partial (consultant-heavy) | **Yes** — positions around revenue generation |
| **Sfridoo (Italy)** | Freemium + Pro tier | Yes (Italy only) | Implicit — sellers get offers |
| **FLOOW2 (NL)** | Subscription, undisclosed | Yes (cluster-based) | **Yes** — SharingScan calculates financial + sustainability benefits upfront |
| **NISP (UK)** | Free (defunct) | Was yes | Yes retrospectively — £1.1B in cost reductions tracked |
| **Kalundborg Symbiosis (DK)** | Publicly funded, not SaaS | No (geographic co-location) | Yes at cluster level |
| **Waste-Outlet / Material Trader** | Subscription | Trader-focused, not SMEs | Market price discovery only |

**Key insights:**
- **NISP proved the financial case** for industrial symbiosis for SMEs (£1B in sales, £1.1B cost reductions, 45M tonnes diverted). No digital platform has replicated it.
- **FLOOW2's SharingScan** is the only pre-commitment financial calculator in the entire landscape — calculates benefits before you join. Almost no other platform does this.
- **No EU-wide waste marketplace** exists. Regional only: Sfridoo (Italy), FLOOW2 (Netherlands), Symbiosis Center (Denmark).

## Category 3: Product Lifecycle / Design for Circularity

| Tool | Pricing | SME Fit | Notes |
|------|---------|---------|-------|
| **SimaPro Craft** | €6,100–€7,800/yr + database costs | None — requires LCA specialist | Industry-standard LCA, expert-gated |
| **OpenLCA** | Free (databases extra ~€hundreds/yr) | Technically yes, expertise-gated | Professional-grade but steep learning curve |
| **One Click LCA** | Quote-based (~€1.5K–€4K+/yr) | Construction sector only | Best-in-class for building circularity |
| **Madaster** | Quote/pilot-based | No (construction) | Material passports increase residual value 15–25% |
| **Circularise** | Enterprise, undisclosed | No | DPP / traceability for regulated supply chains |
| **Materiom** | Free (open-source) | Yes (research only) | 20K+ bio-based material recipes |

**Key insight:** Madaster's framing — "material passports increase residual value by 15–25%" — is powerful and underused outside construction. The financial framing of circularity through asset/material value is the right narrative but locked in a niche.

## Category 4: Circular Business Model Tools

| Tool | Pricing | SME Fit | Notes |
|------|---------|---------|-------|
| **circuly** | Undisclosed SaaS | Partial | PaaS operations (billing, returns, repair). Assumes you already decided to do PaaS. |
| **LoopOS** | Undisclosed (€3M funding) | Partial (retail-scale) | "Circular ERP" — tracks products through trade-in/refurb/resale. Serves Fnac, Decathlon. |
| **Rheaply** | Freemium + subscription | No (US, institutional) | Asset reuse marketplace. $2.4M saved reported. |
| **Firmhouse / Koorvi / ReverseLogix** | Quote-based / enterprise | Partial | Subscription management and reverse logistics |

**Key insight:** No tool helps an SME evaluate whether PaaS/servitization makes financial sense **before** building the capability. Every tool assumes the decision is already made.

## Category 5: EU Regulatory Compliance

| Tool | Pricing | SME Fit | Notes |
|------|---------|---------|-------|
| **Fluxy.One** | Undisclosed, explicitly SME-targeted | Yes | DPP platform — upload docs, get Digital Product Passport with QR code in 27 EU languages. No-code. |
| **Climatiq** | API-based, developer pricing | No | Carbon calculation + DPP guidance |

**Key insight:** Fluxy.One is the only explicitly SME-targeted DPP tool, but it's compliance-only — no circular opportunity identification.

## Stack 3 Gaps Summary

### Gap 1: No Self-Serve Circular Opportunity Scanner for SMEs
Every tool is either enterprise-priced/consulting-gated or free but requiring expert knowledge. No "enter your waste streams, see what they might be worth and who might buy them" tool exists. NISP proved this is valuable (£1.1B in cost reductions for SMEs) but did it via expensive facilitated workshops.

### Gap 2: No Platform Connects Circular Initiatives to Financial Outcomes
FLOOW2's SharingScan is the only pre-commitment financial calculator in the landscape. No waste marketplace offers "here is the estimated annual value of your waste stream" before onboarding.

### Gap 3: No EU-Wide Industrial Symbiosis Matchmaking
Regional platforms only — Sfridoo (Italy), FLOOW2 (Netherlands), Symbiosis Center (Denmark). No EU-wide platform operates a live buyer/seller marketplace for industrial by-products accessible to SMEs.

### Gap 4: No Circular Evidence Portfolio for Tenders
SMEs increasingly need to document circular practices for procurement bids, B Corp certification, ISO 14001, and EU Green Deal compliance. No platform helps build and maintain a circular evidence portfolio exportable as a supplier circular credentials report.

### Gap 5: Design for Circularity Is Construction-Only or Expert-Only
One Click LCA and Madaster are excellent but construction-specific. SimaPro and OpenLCA require LCA expertise. No tool guides a general manufacturing SME through product design circularity assessment, scored and financially framed.

### Gap 6: No Bridge Between Assessment and Action
Tools either measure (Circulytics, CTI, MCI) or transact (Sfridoo, FLOOW2, Rheaply). Nothing bridges: "you scored X on circularity → here are three initiatives with estimated ROI → here is who could buy your waste → here is the documentation for your next tender."

## Stack 3 Positioning

**The unoccupied position:** A self-serve circular opportunity engine for SMEs that (1) diagnoses which circular pathways apply to a specific operation, (2) quantifies the financial upside (revenue + cost avoidance + tender advantage), (3) helps execute (find buyers, model PaaS, document initiatives), and (4) produces audit-ready circular credentials for tenders and customer requirements.

**Closest competitors to watch:** Excess Materials Exchange (right concept, consultant-heavy), FLOOW2 (has the financial calculator idea), LoopOS (circular ERP, retail-scale).

# Stack 4 — Resilience (Stability)

> Systems must flex, not just endure.

## The White Space

The resilience/risk management market is ~$14.1B (projected by 2033, 15.2% CAGR) and almost entirely enterprise-grade. Every category — supply chain risk, business continuity, climate risk, scenario planning — is designed for Fortune 500 procurement teams and dedicated risk functions. SMEs are an afterthought.

| **Everstream Analytics** | Custom, ~$50K–$250K/yr est. | None | ESG events as disruption signals |
| **Interos** | Custom, ~$80K–$500K/yr est. | None | ESG is 1 of 6 risk dimensions |

## Stack 4 Gaps Summary

### Gap 1: Nobody Serves SMEs
The sub-$5K SME resilience market is entirely unserved by purpose-built software. What exists: free government frameworks (no technology), generalist financial planning tools (no risk data), EcoVadis (ESG credentialing, not resilience).

### Gap 2: Nobody Combines Operational + Sustainability Resilience
No tool says: "Your carbon dependency on coal energy creates a climate risk (energy price volatility) AND a sustainability risk (carbon pricing, regulatory exposure) AND a resilience risk (single point of failure in energy source)." The concept "your ESG gaps ARE your resilience gaps" is entirely unaddressed.

### Gap 3: No Self-Serve Scenario Planning for SMEs
No tool accepts an SME's operational reality (suppliers, revenue concentration, energy dependencies) and runs structured what-if scenarios without a consultant or risk team. This is the "TurboTax for risk management" gap.

### Gap 4: Enterprise vs. SME Data Requirements

| Dimension | Enterprise Tool | What SMEs Need |
|---|---|---|
| Data source | External feeds + ERP integration | Own supplier list, own inputs |
| Setup | Months, consultants | Days, self-serve |
| Pricing | $50K–$500K/yr | €300–€1,500/yr |
| Outputs | Risk dashboards, alerts, scores | Action plan, priority list |
| Expertise | Risk management team | Owner-operator |
| Depth | 10-tier supplier mapping | Top 5–10 suppliers |
| Climate risk | Asset portfolio modeling | "Is my supplier in a flood zone?" |

### Gap 5: No Resilience Documentation for Financing/Insurance
No tool produces an investor-ready resilience report for an SME. Large companies use Fusion/Riskonnect for regulators. SMEs have nothing they could walk into a bank meeting with.

### Gap 6: Nobody Uses the SME's Own Data
Enterprise tools integrate with ERPs. SMEs have QuickBooks exports, supplier spreadsheets, energy bills. No tool ingests this lightweight data for scenario modeling.

## Stack 4 Positioning

**The unoccupied position:** Guided, self-serve vulnerability mapping and scenario planning for SMEs, using their own operational data, connecting sustainability gaps to resilience gaps, producing documentation for banks and insurers.

**Closest competitor to watch:** Sphera (combines sustainability + operational risk, enterprise-only), Cervest/Mitiga (climate risk for CSRD, but reporting not planning).

# Stack 5 — Regeneration (Positioning)

> Advantage compounds when it's built into operations.

## The White Space

Dozens of platforms exist for individual pieces of the positioning puzzle — reporting, certifications, supply chain ratings, financing, claims verification — but **no single platform connects operational sustainability proof to market competitive advantage in one workflow for SMEs.**

## Category 1: Certification Readiness

| Tool | Pricing | SME Fit | Proof→Positioning? |
|------|---------|---------|-------------------|
| **B Lab / BIA** | Free assessment; cert fees €1K–€10K+/yr | Yes | No — tells you where you stand, doesn't help leverage it |
| **Apiday** | Custom/enterprise | Partial | Moderate — multi-cert from one dataset, but stops at compliance |
| **diginexESG** | $99/mo | Yes | No — affordable reporting, but compliance-only |

### Gap: BIA is the most holistic assessment (5 dimensions, progressive Year 1/3/5) but is a certification framework, not a positioning engine. No tool bridges "your score is 92/200" to "here's how to turn that into premium pricing."

## Category 2: Sustainability Ratings for Procurement

| Tool | Pricing | SME Fit | Proof→Positioning? |
|------|---------|---------|-------------------|
| **EcoVadis** | ~$339–$2,979/yr (supplier-side) | Yes (demand-driven) | Partial — Gold/Silver rating unlocks RFPs, but stays inside procurement systems |
| **Sedex / SMETA** | £215/yr + audit fees | Yes | No — hygiene requirement, not differentiator |
| **CDP Supply Chain** | Free if requested; $3.1K–$7.3K/yr voluntary | Yes | No — A-list is recognized but not designed as marketing claim |
| **IntegrityNext** | Free for suppliers; €9.8K/yr buyer-side | Yes | No — buyer tool, not supplier positioning |
| **Worldfavor** | Custom | Partial | No — data infrastructure, not positioning |

### Gap: EcoVadis is the closest to proof→procurement advantage (Gold rating literally qualifies you for contracts). But the rating stays inside procurement systems. No tool helps you turn a Gold rating into a premium pricing story, talent attraction narrative, or financing case.

## Category 3: Green Claims & Storytelling

| Tool | Pricing | SME Fit | Proof→Positioning? |
|------|---------|---------|-------------------|
| **Provenance** | Custom, £149+/mo/language | Partial (B2C brands) | **YES — the only tool that bridges verified proof to consumer marketing.** But narrow: consumer goods only, no B2B/procurement/financing. |

### Key insight: Provenance is the only tool in the entire landscape that explicitly links verified sustainability proof to market communication. But it's narrow (consumer goods) and doesn't address procurement, talent, or financing.

### EU Green Claims Directive (ECGT) — enforcement September 2026
Bans generic environmental claims and offset-based "climate neutral" labels. Every claim must be substantiated. No tool helps SMEs audit their current claims for ECGT compliance and replace non-compliant claims with verified alternatives. This is a regulatory catalyst for Stack 5.

## Category 4: Sustainability-Linked Financing

| Tool | Pricing | SME Fit | Notes |
|------|---------|---------|-------|
| **SustainSME** | Not disclosed (finance-provider-facing) | Passive — SME marketplace | Only platform connecting SME sustainability performance to financing access |
| **EU Platform on Sustainable Finance** | Proposal stage (March 2025) | Future standard | Proposes simplified SME framework for green loan eligibility |

### Gap: SustainSME addresses financing access but is provider-facing, not SME self-serve. The EU SME Sustainable Finance Standard is a proposal, not a product. Greenly claims CDP data "strengthens green finance applications" but has no actual financing module.

## Category 5: Ecolabels & Product Certifications

| Certification | Pricing | SME Fit | Positioning Help? |
|--------------|---------|---------|------------------|
| **EU Ecolabel** | €200–€600 application (SME discount) + 0.15% of sales annually | Yes | None — badge only, no positioning engine |
| **Cradle to Cradle** | Contact required, consultant-dependent | No | None — certification only |
| **Regenified / Land to Market** | Variable | Yes (agriculture) | None — niche |
| **Fair Trade** | All-in fee model | Yes | Moderate — premium price for producers |

## Stack 5 Gaps Summary

### Gap 1: "From Data to Premium Pricing"
No tool takes a company's operational ESG data across all categories and builds a premium pricing justification narrative. Consumers spend $217B annually on eco-friendly products (2025), projected $400B+ by 2032. But only 20% of consumers fully trust sustainability claims.

### Gap 2: "Multiple Certifications from One Dataset"
Apiday and diginexESG partially address this (multi-framework data collection). But no platform maps existing operational data to gap analysis across B Corp + EU Ecolabel + EcoVadis + CDP simultaneously with prioritized action plans.

### Gap 3: "Proof Connects to Procurement Positioning"
EcoVadis is closest — Gold/Silver rating genuinely unlocks RFPs. But no tool takes a company's specific sustainability proof across multiple dimensions and builds a tailored preferred-supplier qualification strategy.

### Gap 4: "Internal Proof → External Market Positioning"
Current tools operate in one of two modes:
- Internal data/compliance (EcoVadis, CDP, Greenly, Plan A)
- External claims/marketing (Provenance, certification badges)
The bridge — taking internally-verified operational data and structuring it as external competitive positioning across procurement, consumer marketing, financing, and talent — does not exist.

### Gap 5: "Financing Access from Operational Proof"
No platform takes an SME's operational sustainability data and connects it to sustainability-linked loan applications or green bond access.

### Gap 6: "Greenwashing-Safe Claims Engine"
EU ECGT Directive (September 2026) makes defensible, evidence-backed claims legally mandatory. No tool helps SMEs audit claims for ECGT compliance and replace them with verified, data-backed alternatives.

### Gap 7: "Sustainability Employer Brand"
70% of young professionals factor environmental responsibility into job choice. 25% higher likelihood of attracting top talent with strong sustainability programs (HBR). No tool translates verified sustainability credentials into employer brand narratives.

## Stack 5 Positioning

**The unoccupied position:** The positioning engine for proven sustainable operators — connecting Stacks 1–4 operational proof to multi-certification readiness, procurement qualification, verified claims, financing access, and employer brand.

**Closest competitors to watch:** Provenance (claims→marketing, but B2C only), Apiday (multi-cert data, but compliance not positioning), Aclymate (certification→contract wins angle).
