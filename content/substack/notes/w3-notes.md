## Article Promo

Every carbon accounting tool on the market will give you a number. "Your emissions are 142 tCO2e." Great. Now what? The entire SME sustainability software market is built around measurement. Nobody builds what comes after. This week: why fixing leaks beats adding initiatives, every time.

---

## Note 1

The five stacks in money terms: See your money. Save money. Keep more money. Protect your money. Find more money. Notice what's missing from that list? The word "sustainability." It doesn't need to be there. It's the output, not the objective.

---

## Note 2

The right model for efficiency-as-carbon-reduction already exists. Carbmee built it — MACC curves that rank actions from cheapest to most expensive per tonne reduced. Brilliant. Enterprise-only. Starting above 50,000 euros a year. The 99% of European businesses that actually need it get nothing.

---

## Note 3

Efficiency isn't a sustainability initiative. It's good business. When energy prices spike, the company using 25% more energy per unit than it needs feels the hit 25% harder. Modern sustainability doesn't start with saving the planet. It starts with stopping the leaks.
