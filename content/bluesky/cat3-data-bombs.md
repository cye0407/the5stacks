# Category 3: Data Bombs

Cross-posting note: For Threads and Mastodon, add hashtags: #sustainability #business #operations #modernSustainability

---

**21.** 73% of financial institutions offer green financing for SMEs. 2.8% of SMEs have applied. The money is sitting there. Nobody built the bridge.

**22.** The resilience/risk management market is worth $14.1B and growing at 15% per year. The number of those tools an SME can actually afford: roughly zero.

**23.** $789 billion. That's the green growth opportunity for SMEs identified by ICC and Sage. The industry's response? More carbon calculators.

**24.** 70% of manufacturers expect circular solutions to increase revenue by 2027. 45% haven't started a single circular initiative. Everybody sees the money. Nobody knows how to pick it up.

**25.** The Ellen MacArthur Foundation shut down its own circularity assessment tool. Too expensive to run at scale. If the world's leading circular economy organization can't make assessment work, maybe assessment isn't the answer.

**26.** NISP generated £1 billion in new sales and £1.1 billion in cost reductions by matching one company's waste to another's input. Then the government defunded it. Nobody built the digital replacement. The gap is still there.

**27.** The cheapest credible supply chain risk tool costs $17,000 per year. If you're an SME with three suppliers and one facility, your option is: worry quietly.

**28.** 319,148 businesses are using B Lab's tools. Only 9,576 are certified. That's a 97% dropout rate between interest and certification. The process is the barrier, not the motivation.

**29.** Consumers spend $217B annually on eco-friendly products. But only 20% fully trust sustainability claims. There's a quarter-trillion dollar market waiting for anyone with actual proof.

**30.** The EU Green Claims Directive hits in September 2026. Every unsubstantiated sustainability claim becomes illegal. Most companies haven't started preparing. The scramble will be spectacular.
