How the process works

Seven sessions. One kick-off, five stacks, one wrap-up. Each builds on the last.

0

Kick-off

Review the questionnaire, align on goals, identify the biggest pressure points, and set the agenda for the five stacks.

Output: shared understanding + engagement roadmap
1

Build the baseline

Map your operational data — energy, materials, water, labor, costs. Establish a single source of truth you can defend.

Output: operational baseline + cost-per-unit model
2

Identify the leaks

Analyze where money is leaving the business. Energy waste, process inefficiency, supplier overcharges, hidden cost drivers.

Output: prioritized efficiency plan with projected savings
3

Recover margin

Turn waste streams into value. Identify what you’re discarding that someone else would pay for or that you could reuse.

Output: margin recovery opportunities quantified
4

Map your fragility

Identify supply chain dependencies, concentration risks, and single points of failure before they break.

Output: supply chain risk map + resilience priorities
5

Install the compounding system

Connect the stacks into a feedback loop your team can maintain. The system improves as it operates.

Output: 6–12 month roadmap + internal operating system
6

Wrap-up

Review everything built, confirm the roadmap, hand over ownership. Your team has a system they can run and improve on their own.

Output: final deliverables package + internal handover

Common questions

Is this sustainability consulting?

No. It’s operations work. Sustainability data and environmental proof come out of the system because the business becomes more legible and efficient.

Why is the Full Build emphasized?

Because it’s the complete version. It’s the offer for teams who don’t want to guess, wander, or stall out after a nice workshop.

Can I start smaller?

Yes. Foundation and Alignment are real offers. But they are lower-support paths, not equivalent paths dressed up nicely.

What do I need to prepare?

For Foundation: nothing, it’s self-guided. For Alignment and above: you’ll receive a questionnaire and homework. You bring your real numbers. We work with real data, not estimates.

What kind of businesses is this for?

SMEs with 10–250 employees. Manufacturing, logistics, food production, services — any business where operational costs are a significant part of the picture.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. Your investment in a lower tier is credited toward the next one.
