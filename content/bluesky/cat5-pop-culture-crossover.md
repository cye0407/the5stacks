# Category 5: Pop Culture / Business Crossover

Cross-posting note: For Threads and Mastodon, add hashtags: #sustainability #business #operations #modernSustainability

---

**41.** Every company has a gym membership it never uses. For most, that's their sustainability programme.

**42.** Sustainability right now is where digital transformation was in 2008. Everyone knows it matters. Nobody agrees on what it means. The vendors are making a fortune on confusion.

**43.** Netflix didn't kill Blockbuster by making better DVDs. It changed the operating model. Sustainability won't be solved by better reports. It'll be solved by better operations.

**44.** Your sustainability strategy has the same problem as most New Year's resolutions: big ambition, no system, abandoned by March.

**45.** We don't call it "safety sustainability" when a factory installs guardrails. We just call it good operations. That's where all of sustainability is headed.

**46.** If your sustainability plan can't survive a budget review, it wasn't a plan. It was a wish.

**47.** Imagine if accounting worked like sustainability: hire a consultant once a year, guess at the numbers, publish a report nobody audits, and call it financial management.

**48.** Toyota didn't need a Chief Sustainability Officer to build the most efficient car factories on earth. They needed a system. The sustainability fell out of the system.

**49.** "We'll be net zero by 2040" is the corporate equivalent of "I'll start going to the gym on Monday." We all know how that ends.

**50.** Every startup founder knows you ship fast and iterate. Every sustainability team builds a five-year plan and prays. One of these approaches works.
