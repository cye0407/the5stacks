# W2 Friday — LinkedIn Post

Two suppliers. Similar price. Similar quality.

One sends a link to their verified environmental data within the hour.

The other says they'll need a couple of weeks to pull the numbers together.

Which one do you trust more?
Which one is easier to work with?
Which one keeps the contract?

That difference is already showing up in procurement decisions. Not in five years. Now.

The sustainability questionnaire used to be optional. A nice-to-have from your customer's sustainability team.

It's becoming a contract prerequisite.

Not because buyers suddenly care more about the planet. Because their auditor needs a number in a cell — and that number comes from your operation.

The supplier who can answer keeps the business. The one who can't gets replaced. Not because their product is worse. Because they're harder to report on.

The good news: the data you need is already in your filing cabinet. You just need a structured way to pull it together.
