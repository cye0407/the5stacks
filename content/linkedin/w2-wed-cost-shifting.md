# W2 Wednesday — LinkedIn Post

The biggest companies in Europe made sustainability commitments.

Then they needed to prove it. Not just their own operations — their entire supply chain. Scope 3. That's where 70-90% of emissions actually live.

So they wrote questionnaires. Long ones. Jargon-heavy. Designed for their reporting framework, not for your business.

They put your name at the top and sent it downstream.

Fill it in. Figure it out. Or we won't choose you next time.

They have a sustainability department of twelve. You have Svenja. And Svenja has a real job to do.

This isn't supply chain engagement. It's cost-shifting. The reporting burden flows downhill. The resources to handle it don't.

The uncomfortable truth: understanding this changes how you respond to it.

Because the data they're asking for isn't exotic. It's in your utility bills, your waste invoices, your purchase orders. You've been generating it for years.

You just never had a reason to organise it.

That reason just arrived in your inbox.
