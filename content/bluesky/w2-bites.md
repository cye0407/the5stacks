# W2 Bluesky Bites

## Bite 1
A sustainability questionnaire is a Fortune 500 company asking you to do their homework.

## Bite 2
Scope 3 emissions is a fancy way of saying "we want to count YOUR pollution as OUR progress."

## Bite 3
The company asking you to report your carbon footprint has 200 people in their sustainability department. You have Svenja.

## Bite 4
"Supply chain engagement" is what large companies call it when they outsource the hardest part of sustainability to the businesses least equipped to handle it.

## Bite 5
47-page sustainability questionnaire, translated:
- How much electricity do you use? (Check your bill)
- What do you throw away? (Ask your waste hauler)
- Where does your water go? (Check your water bill)

That's it. That's what they want.
