Stack 2: Efficiency

Smart tariffs are turning energy from cost center to profit center — if you can manage the complexity

Businesses can now earn money by shifting electricity usage patterns, but it requires operational precision.

UK's National Energy System Operator introduced rules allowing businesses and households to earn money by adjusting electricity usage patterns. Smart tariffs are transforming energy procurement as grids become more decentralized. (https://www.edie.net/uk-businesses-and-households-to-reap-rewards-from-flexible-energy-use/)

What this means for your operations

Energy is shifting from a fixed cost to a dynamic revenue opportunity. But this only works if you can flex production schedules, shift equipment runtime, or store energy during cheap periods. Companies with rigid operations will pay premium rates while flexible competitors earn money from the same grid.

The tactic

Map which of your processes can run during off-peak hours (nights, weekends) versus which must run during peak demand periods.
