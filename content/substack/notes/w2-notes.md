## Article Promo

The biggest companies in Europe made sustainability commitments. Then they sent the reporting burden downstream. You're not being asked to be sustainable. You're being asked to do their reporting. I wrote about what that means — and why the data they want is already in your filing cabinet.

---

## Note 1

"Supply chain engagement" is what large companies call it when they push the hardest part of sustainability onto the companies with the smallest budgets. Cost-shifting with better branding.

---

## Note 2

A sustainability questionnaire takes roughly the same time to fill out whether you have 15 employees or 15,000. Only one of those businesses has a team for it.

---

## Note 3

The data they're asking you to report? You're already sitting on most of it. Energy bills. Waste invoices. Purchase orders. Water bills. You've been generating it for years. You just never had a reason to organise it that way.
