## Article Promo

Between 2005 and 2016, a UK programme called NISP generated one billion pounds in new sales and 1.1 billion in cost reductions — just by asking companies: "Does anyone need what you're throwing away?" Then the government defunded it. Nobody built the digital replacement. This week: why what you discard is lost margin.

---

## Note 1

Every waste stream in your operation has two costs. The obvious one: disposal. The hidden one: the value inside it. Material that could be someone else's input. Heat that could warm another process. Byproducts you're literally paying to throw into a landfill. Flip that frame, and the numbers look very different.

---

## Note 2

70% of manufacturers expect circular solutions to increase revenue by 2027. 45% haven't scaled a single circular initiative. The gap isn't motivation — it's the inability to see the financial case before committing. That's the piece nobody has built for SMEs yet.
