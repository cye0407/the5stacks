## Eventbrite Listing

### Title
Margin Protection: How to Stop Profit Erosion and Map Your Operational Costs

### Summary
A free 60-minute workshop for SME owners to stop profit erosion. Map your operational costs, calculate cost-per-unit, and identify exactly where rising costs are hitting your margins. Practical, hands-on, and no pitch.

### Description

**Your costs are rising. You already know that.**

Energy. Materials. Logistics. Every line item is creeping up — and your margins are absorbing the hit.

But here's the thing most business owners don't have time to figure out: **where exactly is the money going?**

You know your total energy bill. But do you know the cost-per-unit of your core processes? You know margins are tighter. But can you point to the three biggest drains and rank them?

Most businesses with 10–250 employees don't have an operations analyst or a consultant on retainer. So these patterns stay invisible — and every price spike hits harder than it should.

**This workshop changes that in 60 minutes.**

#### What you'll walk away with:

- A clear map of your top operational cost categories — and which ones you have poor visibility into
- A rough cost-per-unit calculation for at least one core process, using your own real numbers
- 2–3 specific areas where profit is eroding that you can act on immediately
- A prioritized first step you can take within 7 days

#### This is for you if:

- You own or manage operations at a business with 10–250 employees
- Your business has real operational costs — energy, materials, production, logistics, facilities
- You're feeling the squeeze from rising input costs and shrinking margins
- You don't have a dedicated operations analyst or consultant doing this for you

#### This is NOT for you if:

- You already track cost-per-unit across your processes
- You have a full-time operations or efficiency team
- You're looking for sustainability certification or compliance guidance (that's not what this is)

#### Format:

- Live virtual session via Jitsi Meet (secure, browser-based — no software or account needed)
- Small group — max 12 participants
- Interactive: I recommend having a recent energy bill or supplier invoice nearby to get the most out of our live mapping exercise
- Camera on encouraged, but not mandatory

#### About the facilitator:

Cat Yeldi has spent over a decade working with businesses across sectors — from agriculture to manufacturing — helping them see what their operational data is actually telling them. She built the 5 Stacks Framework to give small and mid-sized businesses the same operational visibility that large corporations pay consultants six figures for.

This isn't a sustainability lecture. It's a practical, hands-on session about your money and where it's going.

**12 seats. One hour. Bring your numbers.**
